# Seftekra Home — Ad Readiness Package

**Goal**: make `/seftekra-home` a self-contained lead-capture funnel for paid traffic on Google Ads, Meta, and TikTok in Africa/emerging markets. No GDPR consent banner required (emerging-market targeting), but privacy policy must disclose tracking pixels (all three platforms require it).

## 1. Lead capture: `HomeLeadForm` + on-page placement

**`src/components/HomeLeadForm.tsx`** (client) — mirrors the existing ContactForm's Web3Forms pattern and animated success/error states:
- Fields: Name, Phone (`type="tel"`), Email, Location (city/area), Home status (Own/Rent), Preferred path (Pay in full / 25% + tariff / Not sure)
- Hidden: `access_key`, `subject: "Seftekra Home Lead"` (so home leads are distinguishable from B2B inquiries in the inbox), UTM + click-ID params (see below), landing page
- On success → fires `trackLead()` (see §2)

**Placement on `/seftekra-home`**:
- New `id="get-started"` section near the bottom (before CrossLinks), styled like ContactForm
- `CtaBanner` gains optional `href` prop (default `/#contact` preserved) — product page passes `#get-started` so the CTA scrolls to the on-page form, **not** the homepage bounce
- `PayStructure` gets a small "Get started" anchor under the two cards → `#get-started`
- **`StickyCta.tsx`** — mobile-only sticky bottom bar ("Get Your Free Assessment →") scrolling to `#get-started`; hidden on desktop. Most African consumer traffic is mobile.

## 2. Tracking: three pixels, env-gated, zero-impact until configured

**`src/components/Analytics.tsx`** (client) — loaded once in `layout.tsx` via `next/script` (`afterInteractive`). Renders Meta Pixel, Google gtag, and TikTok pixel snippets — **each only if its env ID is set**. With no IDs configured, renders nothing; the site behaves exactly as today.

**`src/lib/analytics.ts`** — `trackLead()` helper with existence guards:
- Meta: `fbq('track', 'Lead')`
- Google: `gtag('event', 'generate_lead')` (mark as key event/conversion in the Ads/GA UI)
- TikTok: `ttq.track('Lead')`

**UTM capture** (lead quality gold): on mount, `HomeLeadForm` reads `utm_source / utm_medium / utm_campaign / gclid / fbclid / ttclid` from the URL, persists to sessionStorage (so a visitor who arrives via ad, browses, then submits still attributes correctly), and submits them as hidden fields — every lead email shows which campaign produced it.

**`.env.example`** documenting: `NEXT_PUBLIC_WEB3FORMS_KEY`, `NEXT_PUBLIC_SITE_URL` (existing) + `NEXT_PUBLIC_META_PIXEL_ID`, `NEXT_PUBLIC_GA_MEASUREMENT_ID`, `NEXT_PUBLIC_TIKTOK_PIXEL_ID` (new; set in Cloudflare Pages build env — `NEXT_PUBLIC_*` are build-time inlined).
